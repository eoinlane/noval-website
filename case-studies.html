Case Study 01

AI-Powered EdTech Platform for Irish Secondary Education

Helping Diotima build a curriculum-aligned AI tutoring platform purpose-built for the Leaving Certificate — where generic AI tools consistently fall short on Irish context, language, and assessment rubrics.

Background

Diotima set out to build an AI-powered educational platform tailored to the Irish secondary school curriculum, specifically targeting Leaving Certificate alignment. The challenge was significant: Ireland's curriculum is highly specific, and generic AI tools consistently fail on Irish historical context, Irish language content, and the particular assessment rubrics used by the State Examinations Commission.

What We Did

  • Curriculum Alignment Architecture

    Advised on how to structure content ingestion and retrieval so the platform could accurately map questions and learning objectives to specific Leaving Cert syllabus strands — a problem that required custom approaches rather than off-the-shelf RAG.

  • Question & Rubric Generation

    Worked through the model selection and prompting strategy for generating exam-style questions and marking rubrics, including how to handle the nuance of Irish history and Irish language requirements that generic models handle poorly.

  • Teacher Pilot Programme

    Supported the design of an early pilot with Irish teachers, helping define what "good" looked like for AI-generated content in an Irish classroom context, and how to gather structured feedback to improve the system.

  • Content Licensing Strategy

    As the platform matured, content licensing with Irish publishers became a key risk. I helped identify this early and advised on how to build the platform in a way that reduced dependency on third-party licensed material.

  • Investor Narrative

    Contributed to how the platform's differentiation — Irish-specific, curriculum-aligned, teacher-validated — was articulated for investor conversations.

Outcome

The platform moved from concept to working pilot with Irish secondary school teachers. The team developed a clear product thesis distinguishing it from generic AI tutoring tools, grounded in the specific regulatory and pedagogical context of the Irish education system.

The curriculum alignment approach provided a defensible technical moat — a platform built not just on AI capability, but on deep domain specificity that competitors cannot easily replicate.
