Brand Guidelines

Noval Consulting

A complete reference for the Noval visual identity. These guidelines ensure consistency across all touchpoints — website, social media, presentations, and print.

1. Logo

The Noval identity consists of two components: the icon mark (circular wave motif) and the wordmark. The icon is inspired by Nohoval Cove — flowing wave forms within a circle, representing AI's vast potential guided with purpose.

Icon Mark

On dark backgrounds, the icon is inverted to white using CSS filter. SVG version scales to any size without quality loss.

Icon + Wordmark

Noval
Noval

Wordmark Only

Do

  • Use icon + wordmark wherever space allows
  • Use the icon mark alone for favicons, social avatars, and small contexts
  • Maintain clear space equal to the icon's height on all sides

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
  • Change the logo colours outside approved variants
  • Place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast

5. Tone of Voice

Noval's voice is authoritative but approachable. We speak from deep enterprise experience but never talk down. We're the trusted guide, not the lecturing professor.

Do

  • "Navigate the EU AI Act with Confidence"
  • "Battle-tested experience in delivering Responsible AI"
  • "Practical, business-led AI solutions"

Don't

  • "We leverage cutting-edge synergies in the AI space"
  • "Our proprietary methodology disrupts the paradigm"
  • Overpromise or use hype language

6. Imagery

Photography should evoke the Irish coastal landscape — natural, uncrowded, contemplative. The hero image (Nohoval Cove) sets the standard: real locations, natural light, no stock-photo polish.

Do

  • Use Irish landscape photography with natural tones
  • Pair images with the green/gold palette
  • Use the wave mark as a watermark on thumbnails

Don't

  • Use generic tech/corporate stock photos
  • Use images with heavy filters or artificial lighting
  • Overcrowd compositions — let the landscape breathe
