Founder & Principal

Eoin Lane

A seasoned AI executive with a proven track record of delivering transformative, enterprise-scale AI solutions. Renowned for leading global teams of data scientists and engineers, Eoin combines cutting-edge AI technologies, design thinking, and engineering excellence to create scalable and responsible AI applications.

Eoin is a recipient of multiple industry awards, including being shortlisted for the prestigious Gartner Eye on Innovation Awards (2020 & 2022). He spearheaded the establishment of a €8 million Digital R&D Hub in Dublin, recognised by IDA Ireland, positioning BNY Mellon at the forefront of AI, data science, and emerging technologies — including quantum computing.

An IBM Master Inventor, Eoin holds over 70 patents and patents pending, alongside several dozen publications, showcasing deep technical expertise and a sustained commitment to innovation.

With a passion for driving impactful AI solutions, Eoin now leads Noval Consultancy, helping businesses unlock the full potential of artificial intelligence while navigating the complexities of compliance and governance.
